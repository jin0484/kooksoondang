BRAND HISTORY

KOOKSOONDANG

  1. 1952-1980

    TRADITION

    Carrying Forward the Heritage of Korean Traditional Brewing

    Kooksoondang builds on Korean brewing methods, including nuruk research and fermentation techniques, to preserve the value of Korean drinking culture.

    Key Points

    • Began brewing business in 1952
    • Established the Korea Microbial Research Institute in 1970
    • Developed traditional nuruk research and fermentation technologies

  2. 1980-1990

    INNOVATION

    Creating New Possibilities Beyond Tradition

    While preserving traditional brewing methods, Kooksoondang expanded the possibilities of Korean alcohol through innovative technologies.

    Key Points

    • Developed and patented the raw rice fermentation method
    • Launched Baekseju
    • Established a new direction for the traditional Korean alcohol market

  3. 1990-2000

    SUSTAINABILITY

    Brewing in Harmony with Nature

    Kooksoondang connects a healthy environment with sustainable brewing practices and eco-friendly production initiatives.

    Key Points

    • Operates a brewery in Hoengseong, Gangwon Province
    • Uses eco-friendly brewing methods through raw rice fermentation technology
    • Recognized for environmental protection efforts

  4. 2000-THE PRESENT

    GLOBAL

    Bringing the Spirit of Korean Alcohol to the Global Stage

    Through traditional liquor restoration projects and global exports, Kooksoondang shares Korean traditional alcohol with people around the world.

    Key Points

    • Conducted traditional liquor restoration projects
    • Expanded Kooksoondang Fresh Makgeolli worldwide
    • Received the USD 7 million Export Tower Award in 2021
