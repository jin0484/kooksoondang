Brand History

Kooksoondang

  1. 1970-1980

    Tradition

    Carrying Forward the Heritage of Korean Traditional Brewing. Founded in 1952 as Kirin Brewery, Kooksoondang has grown through its dedication to traditional Korean brewing methods, including the research of nuruk (traditional fermentation starter) and fermentation techniques.

    By reinterpreting centuries-old fermentation methods in a modern way, Kooksoondang has continued to preserve and share the unique value of Korean drinking culture.

    Key Points

    • Began brewing business in 1952
    • Established the Korea Microbial Research Institute in 1970
    • Developed traditional nuruk research and fermentation technologies

  2. 1980-1990

    Innovation

    Creating New Possibilities Beyond Tradition. While preserving traditional brewing methods, Kooksoondang has continuously expanded the possibilities of Korean alcohol through innovative technologies.

    With the development of the raw rice fermentation method in 1982, Kooksoondang achieved a cleaner and deeper flavor, and introduced a new standard for traditional Korean liquor with the launch of Baekseju in 1992.

    Key Points

    • Developed and patented the raw rice fermentation method
    • Launched Baekseju
    • Established a new direction for the traditional Korean alcohol market

  3. 1990-2000

    Sustainability

    Brewing in Harmony with Nature. Kooksoondang believes that great alcohol begins with a healthy environment.

    Based in the clean natural surroundings of Hoengseong, Gangwon Province, Kooksoondang continues to practice sustainable brewing methods while pursuing eco-friendly production and ESG initiatives.

    Key Points

    • Operates a brewery in Hoengseong, Gangwon Province
    • Applies eco-friendly brewing methods through raw rice fermentation technology
    • Recognized as a Green Company for environmental protection efforts

  4. 2000-The Present

    Global

    Bringing the Spirit of Korean Alcohol to the Global Stage. Kooksoondang continues to share the beauty of Korean traditional alcohol with the world by reinterpreting its heritage through a modern perspective.

    Through traditional liquor restoration projects and global exports, Kooksoondang introduces the unique culture and value of Korean alcohol to people around the world.

    Key Points

    • Conducted traditional liquor restoration projects
    • Expanded Kooksoondang Fresh Makgeolli worldwide
    • Received the USD 7 million Export Tower Award in 2021
    • Shared the value of Korean traditional alcohol globally
