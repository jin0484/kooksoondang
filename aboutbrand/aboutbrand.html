ABOUT THE KOOKSOONDANG BRAND

Brand history

1970~1985

Providing a strong foundation for Korea's alcoholic beverage industry

through advanced enzyme and coenzyme technologies.

  • 1970 ~ 1982

    Established a research institute and advanced fermentation technology.

  • 1983~1985

    Founded Baehan Industrial Co., Ltd., launched enzymes for alcohol production, and developed a non-steam alcohol production technology.

  • 1986~1990

    Built a stronger production and R&D foundation with the Suwon Plant, a new research institute, and advanced alcohol production technology.

  • 1992~1993

    Rebranded as Kooksoondang, launched fresh rice fermentation makgeolli, and expanded operations through a factory acquisition.

Created new business opportunities through technological excellence.

1986~1993

1994~1999

Adapting tradition to today

  • 1994~1995

    Expanded nationwide and overseas, earning KT certification and establishing a corporate research institute.

  • 1996~1999

    Strengthened product quality with the launch of canned makgeolli and Venture Business and ISO 9002 certifications.

  • 2025

    Reinforced brand competitiveness with the relaunch of Baekseju and a new marketing campaign.

  • ESG Initiatives

    Advanced ESG through traditional brewing preservation, community partnerships, and youth support.
